How it works
  1. Send each judge the scorer link (or a printed scorecard).
  2. Judges score every pitch; scores save in their own browser as they go.
  3. After the last pitch, each judge downloads their CSV and sends it to the organisers.
  4. Organisers open Combined Results and import the 4 CSVs (or type the paper marks).

Privacy: scores are never uploaded. They stay in each person's browser until they download or clear them.
